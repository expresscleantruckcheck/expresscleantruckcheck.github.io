Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Express Clean Truck Check (“I,” “me,” “my”) collects through this website at expresscleantruckcheck.github.io, how I use it, and your choices. I am a mobile Clean Truck Check tester serving Sacramento, California and nearby cities.

Information I collect

When you fill out the registration form, you may give me:

  • Your full name and phone number (required)
  • Your email address (preferred, not required)
  • Your ZIP code (optional)
  • Notes you choose to write, such as vehicle type, where it is parked, and best days and times (optional)
  • Your confirmation that your vehicle is over 14,000 lbs GVWR, runs on diesel or an alternative fuel, and is model year 2013 or newer

If you call or email me, I also receive whatever you tell me in that call or message.

The site also collects basic visit information through Google Analytics, such as the pages you view, your browser and device type, your approximate location, and when you tap the call, email or register buttons. This uses cookies. It does not tell me who you are.

How I use it

  • To contact you by phone, text or email about scheduling and your test
  • To keep customer records, so future tests go faster
  • To send invoices and take payment
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it

Who else handles your information

I use a few services to run the site and my business. They only handle your information to provide their service:

  • Square stores my customer list, invoices and payments. Your registration is added there.
  • Cloudflare passes your registration from the website to Square.
  • GitHub hosts the website.
  • Google provides Analytics and the fonts used on the site. Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

When I perform a Clean Truck Check test, the test results and vehicle information are reported to the California Air Resources Board (CARB) as the program requires.

I do not sell your personal information. I do not share it for advertising. I may share it if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep customer records for as long as I need them to serve you and to meet business and legal requirements. You can ask me to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. I will respond as the law requires.
  • You can block cookies or Google Analytics in your browser settings, or use Google’s opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can skip every optional field on the registration form.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, and I use trusted services to store it. No website or storage method is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is for business owners and operators. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
